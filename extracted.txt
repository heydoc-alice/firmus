[P:Normal] Medical Claims Utilization Report
[P:Normal] June vs July 2026  |  Prepared for HR Management
[P:Normal] Scope: Outpatient (OPC) medical claims, all employees. Entitlement: 14 clinic visits per employee, effective May 2026.
[P:Heading1] 1. Executive Summary
[P:ListParagraph] Total clinic visits fell from 42 in June to 29 in July, a 31% decrease. MC (medical certificate) days issued fell from 34 to 21, a 38% decrease — both consistent with the seasonal respiratory-illness spike easing after June.
[P:ListParagraph] Diseases of the respiratory system remain the top cause of visits but dropped sharply — from 22 visits in June (52% of all June visits) to 6 in July (21%).
[P:ListParagraph] No employee is close to exhausting the 14-visit annual entitlement. The two highest cumulative utilizers (May–July) are each at 6 visits (43% of entitlement).
[P:ListParagraph] 8 employees visited a clinic in both June and July. One of them (FSBH000087) has two chronic (LTM) diagnoses on file — rheumatoid arthritis/polyneuropathy and diabetes/hyperlipidemia — and should be reviewed for a chronic-care / LTM management pathway rather than tracked as routine acute utilization.
[P:ListParagraph] 2 identical rejected reimbursement claims were submitted by the same employee (FSBH000094) on the same date in July, worth a duplicate-submission check with the TPA/insurer.

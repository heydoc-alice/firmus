Findings

Key Findings

Finding 01

Total clinic visits fell from 42 in June to 29 in July, a 31% decrease. MC (medical certificate) days issued fell from 34 to 21, a 38% decrease — both consistent with the seasonal respiratory-illness spike easing after June.

Finding 02

Diseases of the respiratory system remain the top cause of visits but dropped sharply — from 22 visits in June (52% of all June visits) to 6 in July (21%).

Finding 03

No employee is close to exhausting the 14-visit annual entitlement. The two highest cumulative utilizers (May–July) are each at 6 visits (43% of entitlement).

Finding 04 — Review

8 employees visited a clinic in both June and July. One of them (FSBH000087) has two chronic (LTM) diagnoses on file — rheumatoid arthritis/polyneuropathy and diabetes/hyperlipidemia — and should be reviewed for a chronic-care / LTM management pathway rather than tracked as routine acute utilization.
